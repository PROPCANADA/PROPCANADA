🔦 Blacklight Behaviour
Real Bills Don't Glow. Neither Does Ours.

Modern Canadian polymer banknotes contain no UV-fluorescent ink. Under a 365nm blacklight, a genuine bill appears completely dark — this is by design, as the Bank of Canada removed UV security features when it transitioned to the polymer substrate.

Cheap imitations produced with low-grade inks will glow bright blue or white under UV — instantly exposing themselves as poor-quality reproductions. Our prop bills, however, use UV-transparent ink technology that produces zero fluorescence.

With Prop Canada, your prop bill passes the blacklight test — because the real one does too.
